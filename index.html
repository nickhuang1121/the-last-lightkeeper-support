隱私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 23 日。適用於 Nick Huang 提供的 The Last Lightkeeper iOS 與 Android 版。

遊戲進度與雲端存檔

你可以直接遊玩。遊戲會在裝置保存關卡紀錄、設定、剩餘能量與獎勵紀錄。使用平台服務同步時，遊戲會處理平台玩家識別碼，將進度、獎勵紀錄及同步所需識別碼存到 Apple 的 Game Center／iCloud 或 Google Play Games。這些服務依各平台的隱私政策運作。

購買及恢復購買

Apple 和 Google 分別處理其商店的付款。遊戲會取得商品識別碼、交易證明和購買或退款狀態，用來開啟或恢復已購買的權益。付款卡號由商店處理。請在原購買平台使用原商店帳號恢復。

獎勵廣告及隱私選項

遊戲使用 Google AdMob 提供自選的能量補充廣告。Google 及其廣告合作夥伴可能處理 IP 位址、裝置或廣告識別碼、廣告互動和診斷資料，用於廣告放送、成效衡量與防弊。你可以在遊戲內開啟可用的廣告隱私選項，也可以在裝置設定管理廣告或追蹤權限。請參考 Google 如何使用合作網站或應用程式的資料 與 Apple 隱私政策。

保留、刪除與聯絡

本機資料可透過作業系統清除 App 資料，或刪除 App。已同步進度和商店交易可能另外保存在平台帳號中。你可以使用相應的 Apple 或 Google 帳號工具，或聯絡下方支援信箱，詢問資料存取、更正或刪除。聯絡時提供的信箱和訊息會用於處理請求，並只保留至完成支援及履行適用義務所需的期間。

遊戲支援

在遊戲商店選擇「恢復購買」，可重新取得原平台帳號的永久解鎖。遊戲進度同步請登入原本使用的 Game Center 與 iCloud，或 Google Play Games 帳號。

如有問題，請將平台、遊戲版本與發生情況寄到 warhammerlieutenant@gmail.com。請勿寄送密碼或完整付款資料。

Privacy policy and support

Effective November 23, 2026. This policy covers The Last Lightkeeper for iOS and Android, provided by Nick Huang.

Progress and cloud saves

You can play offline. Level progress, settings, energy, and rewards are stored on your device. If you use cloud sync, the game uses your platform player ID and stores your progress, reward records, and related sync identifiers through Apple Game Center and iCloud or Google Play Games. Each provider handles its services under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Apple and Google handle payments on their respective stores. The game uses product IDs, transaction evidence, and purchase or refund status to grant, restore, or revoke entitlements. Your store handles your payment card details. To restore a purchase, use the original account on the store where you bought it.

Rewarded ads and choices

Optional rewarded ads use Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. You can use in-game ad privacy options when available and manage ad or tracking permissions on your device. See how Google uses partner data and Apple’s privacy policy.

Retention, deletion, and support

To remove local data, clear the app data where supported or delete the app. Synced saves and store records may remain in your platform account. Use the platform’s account tools, or email warhammerlieutenant@gmail.com with questions about access, correction, deletion, or gameplay. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Please include your platform and app version, and avoid sending passwords or full payment details.
